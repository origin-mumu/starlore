# Starlore App — Product Context

## What it is
Starlore 的移动端 App，星语治愈系内容平台。用户浏览星座运势文章、与 AI 助手对话、管理个人收藏。

## Register
Product — design serves the task. Users are reading, browsing, chatting. The tool should disappear into the content.

## Color Strategy
Restrained. Warm earth tones from the healing prototype:
- Primary: Sage green (#7A8B76)
- Accent: Terracotta (#D48C70)
- Tinted neutrals leaning warm
- Never pure black or white

## Typography
System font stack (PingFang SC / SF Pro / system-ui). One family, tight scale ratio 1.15.

## Motion
200-300ms state changes. ease-out-expo for entrances. No bounce.

## Anchor References
- 小红书 (content feed layout)
- 微信读书 (reading experience)
- 即刻 (social + AI blend)

## Key Screens
1. 首页 — 文章流 + 星座运势卡片
2. AI 对话 — 聊天界面
3. 文章详情 — 阅读体验
4. 个人中心 — 收藏、设置
